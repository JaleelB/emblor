## Choose your path

- **New install:** read [Introduction](/docs/introduction), then [Installation](/docs/installation).
- **Building a field:** start with [Anatomy](/docs/anatomy), then choose [State management](/docs/guides/state-management) or [Styling](/docs/guides/styling).
- **Hard behavior:** use [Validation](/docs/guides/validation), [Constraints & interactions](/docs/guides/constraints-and-interactions), or [Keyboard & focus](/docs/guides/keyboard-and-focus).
- **Migrating:** use [Migration from v1](/docs/guides/migration) for removed props and composition mappings.
- **Looking for a ready composition:** browse [Playground](/playground) and install a website-owned registry item.
